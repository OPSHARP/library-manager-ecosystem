PART A: PRIVACY POLICY FOR LIBRARY MANAGER AND OWNER (OWNER APP)

1. Introduction

Library Manager and Owner ("we", "us", or "our") provides a comprehensive library management platform for library owners and managers ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Library Manager and Owner application.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Library Manager and Owner is designed with a privacy-first architecture. All data is securely processed and stored using Google Firebase cloud services.

  • Account Information: Profile information via Google Sign-In, including display name, email address, profile picture URL, and unique Google user ID. Authentication is provided exclusively through Google Sign-In to ensure account security.
  • Library Setup Data: Library name, physical address, location coordinates, contact phone number, operating hours, seat capacity, floor plan layouts, logo images, and subscription preferences.
  • Student Management Data: Student names, phone numbers, parent contact details, addresses, assigned seats, fee structures, payment status, join dates, shift timings, and attendance records.
  • Staff Management Data: Staff names, phone numbers, assigned roles, and access permissions.
  • Financial & Inventory Records: Fee records, payment statuses, transaction dates, digital receipt details, book titles, authors, ISBN numbers, and book issue/return logs.
  • Automated Data Processing: Server-side Firebase Cloud Functions process data automatically for fee payment reminders, low-attendance alerts, data aggregation for reports, and scheduled backups.

3. Authentication and Security

Google Sign-In is the exclusive authentication method for the App. We do not collect or store passwords. Firebase App Check with Play Integrity is active to verify that requests originate exclusively from authentic, untampered app instances.

4. Permissions Requested

  • Camera: Scanning QR codes for student attendance, scanning ISBN barcodes for books, and capturing library logos or student photos.
  • Location: Setting and publishing library map location so students can find your library.
  • Storage & Media Access: Exporting PDF, Excel, or CSV reports and backing up data locally.
  • Notifications: Displaying fee alerts, attendance updates, and operational reminders.
  • Biometrics: Fingerprint or face unlock to secure access to the App on your device.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Library Manager and Owner integrates with Google Sign-In (authentication), Google Cloud Firestore (database storage), Firebase Cloud Functions and App Check (server-side processing and security enforcement), Google Mobile Ads / AdMob (displaying non-intrusive advertisements in the free tier), and Google Play In-App Updates (background updates).

6. Data Storage & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. You can delete your account and all associated library data directly in the App under Settings > Delete Account. Upon deletion, all records stored in Cloud Firestore are permanently purged.

PART B: PRIVACY POLICY FOR LM STUDENT APP (STUDENT APP)

1. Introduction

The LM Student App ("Student App") provides students with a self-service portal to interact with their registered library, check seat availability, view fee status, mark attendance, and discover nearby libraries.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Data: Name, email address, phone number, and profile details provided during account setup or via Google Sign-In.
  • Attendance & Activity Logs: Check-in and check-out timestamps, attendance history, assigned seat details, borrowed books, fee payment status, and notification preferences.
  • Location Data: Accesses device location to display nearby libraries on an interactive map. Location data is processed locally on your device and is not continuously tracked or saved.
  • Device Tokens & IDs: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for push notifications and Advertising IDs for Google AdMob ads.

3. Permissions Requested

  • Camera: Scanning library QR codes to check in, check out, or join a library. Camera feeds are processed in real time and no images or videos are stored.
  • Location: Finding nearby libraries on the interactive map.
  • Notifications: Receiving alerts regarding attendance, fee reminders, and library announcements.

4. Third-Party Services

The LM Student App incorporates Google Sign-In, Firebase Services (Cloud Firestore, FCM, App Check), Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, and Google Play In-App Updates.

5. Data Deletion & Rights

Students have the right to request account and data deletion at any time via the Student App settings menu or by contacting drillmovies@gmail.com.

PART C: GENERAL TERMS & CONTACT INFORMATION

1. Children's Privacy

Library Manager and Owner is intended for adult library managers. The LM Student App is intended for students. If a student is a minor under 18 years of age, parent or guardian consent should be obtained prior to registering in a library.

2. Security Measures

All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS), and data at rest is encrypted using Google Cloud encryption standards.
